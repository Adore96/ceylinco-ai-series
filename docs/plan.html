Noob Dev for Ceylinco

AI in Oracle APEX

Three Wednesday sessions for senior technical staff who know Oracle and SQL well and are new to APEX. Week 1 covers APEX and the latest Oracle AI features. Weeks 2 and 3 build two AI workflows live, modelled on Ceylinco's own use cases: garage estimate processing and an intelligent call centre.

Audience
Senior technical leads, strong in Oracle and SQL, new to APEX and AI
Schedule
Wednesdays 25 November, 2 December and 9 December
Format
3.5 hours each; talk and live demos, no laptops needed
Presenter
Kasun Perera

Builds on the 18 November session by a Noob Dev colleague, which covers AI basics and n8n.

Week 2, 2 DecemberCore app and garage estimate AI

What attendees leave with

  • A complete APEX application built in front of them, from tables to a working app.
  • A document AI workflow: a garage estimate image goes in, structured claim data comes out.
  • A pattern for trusting AI output: SQL checks plus a confidence score, with a person approving anything uncertain.

AI reads the document; SQL decides whether the result is good enough; a person signs off when it is not.

Week 3, 9 DecemberIntelligent call centre

What attendees leave with

  • An AI step that recognises what a caller wants (policy, claim or product and branch question) from a call transcript.
  • An AI agent that answers policy and claim questions through approved PL/SQL tools, also exposed as REST APIs.
  • Document question-answering (RAG) built inside the Oracle database with SQL.
  • A view of what changes when this moves to production on their own Oracle environment.

The application

One application for a fictional insurer that sells life and motor cover, built on stage across weeks 2 and 3. All data is synthetic: sample garage estimates, call transcripts and product documents.
